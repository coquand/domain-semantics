Original MIN proof: Unit and Agda check times
===========================================

Measured on 14 November 2026, on the same local macOS arm64 machine.
The two checks ran sequentially. Both completed successfully. These are
single-run measurements with source checking forced, not cold OS-cache
measurements. Neither checker was compiled as part of the timed commands.

Scope: domain-model soundness, logical-relation adequacy, Pi injectivity,
and subject reduction for the original type-in-type MIN development.
This is smaller than the extended Scott-domain development in UNIT/.

0.54 s is elapsed wall time for the complete process.
