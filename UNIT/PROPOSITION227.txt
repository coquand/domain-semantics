Proposition 2.27: corrected rank-stage theorem and printed obstruction
====================================================================

The corrected rank-stage version is now checked in Unit for every n.
The chosen repair starts the compact basis with bottom and U. The
unamended printed rules still have the obstruction recorded below.

Direct fixed-point proof (now checked)
--------------------------------------

Write P_0 = bottom, P_(k+1) = Phi(P_k), p = sup_k P_k, and

  rho_n(Q)(a,x) = r_n(Q(e_n a,e_n x)).

RankProjection proves the structural equations for q_n = e_n r_n. In
particular, application of a stage-(n+1) element has stage-n range and
factors through q_n. Its Pi family has the same properties, and its Pi
domain lies in stage n. These facts use only ranks and the domain equation.

StageFusion proves, for arbitrary continuous binary Q,R:

  rho_0(Phi(Q)) = rho_0(Phi(R));
  rho_n(Q) = rho_n(R) implies rho_(n+1)(Phi(Q)) = rho_(n+1)(Phi(R)).

Thus rho_n(P_k) is constant for k >= n+1. OperationalStages defines

  p-hat_n = rho_n(P_(n+1)),

using only a FINITE unfolding, not the limit p. Continuity of restriction
then proves

  r_n(p(e_n a,e_n x)) = p-hat_n(a,x).

OperationalStages.proposition-2-27 is the resulting identity in D_n.
DirectProjectorStages.p makes the alternative definition

  p_n(a,x) = r_n(p(e_n(a),e_n(x)))

literal in checked Unit code. Its finite-unfolding theorem identifies
this p_n with OperationalStages.p. The definition itself does not use
the bounded typing relation.
StageFusion.eventual and OperationalStages.stabilizes state the stronger
finite-stabilization result. There are no projector hypotheses on Q,R
in the one-step fusion lemma. No unrestricted commutation of p with q_n
on arbitrary arguments is asserted or needed.

This proof does not use the sandwich lemma or the identification of p
with the typing projector. The direct modules are checked BEFORE
StageInterpolation is imported in All.seg.

StageComparison.agreement separately proves p-hat_n = p_n for the
bounded-typing definition below. StageComparison.At.finitary transfers
finitariness to its ambient extension x |-> e_n(p-hat_n(a,r_n x)). This
comparison still uses the earlier sandwich proof. A direct independent
proof by induction that the newly defined p_n(a,-) is finitary is not
claimed. The successor theorem must handle the dependent function clause

  z |-> p_n(f(p_n(a,z)), x(p_n(a,z)))

and the universe/Pi clause simultaneously. Idempotence in the latter
also needs the stage normalization equation

  p_n(p_n(U,a),z) = p_n(a,z).

The existing finite-step-graph basis does not yet supply a checked
closure theorem for this dependent projector construction. That is the
remaining proof obligation for the proposed alternative to inductive
compact typing.

Corrected statement and independent definitions
------------------------------------------------

Use the information order <= in this explanation (Unit's token Le is
the reverse order). Let rank(bottom) = rank(U) = 0, with

  rank(Pi(a,f)) = 1 + max(rank(a), graph-rank(f)),
  rank(a -> b)  = 1 + max(rank(a), rank(b)).

Graph rank assigns a -> b the maximum of the ranks of a and b, without
the extra successor; joins take maxima. Let K_n be the compact classes
having a proper consistent representative of rank at most n. This
existential formulation matters: syntactically equivalent tokens can
have different ranks. K_0 consists exactly of the bottom and U classes.

Define q_n(x) as the supremum of the K_n compacts below x. Let D_n be
its fixed image, e_n its inclusion in D, and r_n(x) = q_n(x), considered
as an element of D_n. The embedding-projection laws and exhaustion of D
are checked.

For proper compact representatives u,b, the bounded MIN judgement is

  u :_n b  iff  rank(u), rank(b) <= n and MIN.Tp(u:b).

In particular bottom :_0 bottom, bottom :_0 U, and U :_0 U all hold.
For A in D_n, define the stage compact family independently by the
typed generators u with u :_n b for some b in A, closing under
compatible finite joins and compact equivalence. Its associated
projector, restricted to D_n, is p_n. Neither this definition nor the
definition of :_n mentions the global p.

For p constructed by the actual Phi/Kleene iteration, Unit now proves

  r_n (p (e_n A) (e_n X)) = p_n A X       (A,X in D_n).

The final equality is in D_n, not merely in the ambient domain D:

  StageCompatibility.proposition-2-27

The bounded-typing proof's interpolation lemma
----------------------------------------------

Suppose L, X, A have proper consistent representatives of rank at most
n. Given an arbitrary compact typing u:b with

  L <= u <= X,    b <= A,

there are proper bounded-rank u',b' with

  u':b',    L <= u' <= X,    b' <= A.

There is deliberately no assertion u' <= u or b' <= b. Such a one-sided
shrinking assertion is too strong. StageInterpolation.interpolate proves
the two-sided statement by induction on n and then on the lower token L.
The graph cases select typed input/output witnesses, select bounded
support from the upper caps, and interpolate the inputs and outputs
together. The Pi case joins the interpolated domains before forming its
typed family; the function case uses a common lower bound assembled
from the supports of both the value graph and its classifier graph.

For compatibility, a compact observation of q_n(p(A,X)) gives such a
lower L. A typed observation of p gives u:b. Since A and X lie in D_n,
their memberships give bounded caps for b and u. Interpolation then
gives a generator of p_n covering L. This proves the difficult inclusion.
The converse uses inclusion of bounded typed generators in global typed
generators, together with the fact that p_n has stage-bounded image.
Finally KleeneProjector.identification transfers the result to the p
defined by iteration rather than typing.

Printed conventions (paper1.pdf, pages 8--9 and 12)
-------------------------------------------------

  C_0 = {bottom},  C_(n+1) = F(C_n).
  u :_n a is a judgement with u,a in C_n.

Figure 2 has these rules, among others:

  a :_n U                 U :_(n+1) U
  --------
  bottom :_n a

  a :_n U, dom(g,a), all values of g :_n U
  ---------------------------------------
  Pi(a,g) :_(n+1) U.

The Lam rule likewise requires a :_n U. There is no stage-zero axiom.
Consequently there is no finite derivation of any stage-zero element
typing judgement: Bot can only recurse into another such derivation, and
the other three rules have successor-stage conclusions.

First-stage calculation
-----------------------

Let z = Pi(bottom,bottom-function). Both U and z belong to C_1, and z is
distinct from bottom because the Pi summand is lifted.

At stage 1, neither Pi nor Lam can apply, since each requires a stage-zero
domain typing. Thus the only possible typed values are bottom and U.
Of these, only bottom lies below z. The paper's typed-generator definition
therefore gives

  p_1(U,z) = bottom.

In contrast, the global recursive equations give

  p(U,z) = Pi(p(U,bottom), lambda x. p(U,bottom)) = z.

The first-stage embedding sends z to z, and restriction fixes this embedded
element. Hence the left side of Proposition 2.27 is z, while its right
side is bottom. The mismatch already affects Proposition 2.18: bottom
:_1 bottom is derivable, but bottom :_0 bottom is not. Also z :_2 U is
derivable whereas z :_1 U is not.

What is checked, and what is not
--------------------------------

StageBaseObstruction.seg introduces a PERMISSIVE skeleton Printed of the
four Figure 2 rules. It retains the predecessor-stage domain typing
premise in Pi and Lam but drops their other premises, and allows ambient
token representatives and conversion. This admits more derivations, not
fewer. It is not a full encoding of the paper's stage-indexed extensional
typing relation. The first-stage absence argument depends only on the
retained premises, so applies also to the literal rules.

The checked declarations are:

  zero-empty / one-shape / pi-untyped
    No zero-stage derivation; first-stage typed values are bottom or U;
    z is not typed at stage 1 even in this permissive relation.

  printed-bottom-bottom-one / printed-pi-two
    The delayed derivations in the permissive relation.
    For the actual rules the omitted premises at these examples hold:
    choose bottom in dom(bottom,bottom), and every value is bottom.

  universe-stage / pi-stage
    Both counterexample arguments are fixed by the existing first-depth
    projection. Their membership in the printed C_1 also follows directly
    from F({bottom}); no general successor-stage isomorphism is used.

  StageProjector / FirstValue.equation
    An independent projector generated by stage-bounded Printed typings,
    with compatible-join closure. Its value at z is bottom, for every
    type argument. Since this is a permissive extension of the literal
    rules, the literal typed-generator supremum cannot have a larger value
    there. Adding bottom or compatible-join closure does not remove the
    obstruction.

  global-observation / lhs-observation / different / not-compatible
    The actual Kleene projector contains the observation z; its first
    restriction retains z; the two stage elements are unequal. Compatibility
    with the independently generated permissive stage projector is refuted.

This is a checked obstruction to the printed conventions, together with
the mathematical comparison above. It must not be reported as a completed
formalisation of the paper's full stage construction or as a proof of
Proposition 2.27.

Possible amendments
-------------------

1. Keep C_0 = {bottom}, but permit the external classifier judgement
   bottom :_0 U. This is an explicit exception to the condition that the

2. Start with C_0 containing bottom and U and the judgement U :_0 U.
   The older MIN rank convention RANK(bottom) = RANK(U) = 0 is compatible
   with this starting point, though it differs from the printed chain.

The second amendment has now been explicitly adopted for the new MIN

bounded-rank interpolation lemma supplies the missing general argument.

The independent fixed-point result
----------------------------------

OperatorContinuity.Phi is a continuous binary operation for every
continuous binary input, and Approximate proves joint directed continuity
in the operation and its two arguments. BinarySup constructs pointwise
directed suprema of continuous binary maps.

KleeneProjector.iterate starts at bottom and applies this actual Phi.
candidate is its pointwise supremum. equation proves Phi(candidate) =
candidate and Below.least proves leastness among pre-fixed binary maps.
Only afterwards are semantic-prefixed, above-semantic and identification
used to compare it with the typed compact-family projector. finitary and
normalization follow. No projector property is assumed to construct the
iterates or their supremum.

This checks the identification route quoted in the conversation. It does
not separately formalise the two finitariness/normalization invariants
for every individual Kleene iterate, nor identify Kleene indices with
domain-stage indices.
