Pi-injectivity via a displayed logical-relation section
======================================================

Agreed status
-------------

Independent Pi-injectivity via a displayed logical-relation section is
formalised. The Artin-glued model formulation is not pursued further;
see "Verdict on the glued-model route" below.

This is the existing Coquand-Huber reducibility argument, organised as a
displayed algebra over derivations and its section. It is a re-checked,
modular presentation, not a new proof: ScottGlueingInversion follows
IndependentInversion step for step (bottom environment, identity
substitutions, observation pi(om,om), DerivationRecovery). What is new is
that the induction is the generic DisplayedSyntax section, and that the
assembled IndependentFundamental module is not imported.

The checked inversion does not use the comma category, an already
assembled fundamental lemma, or an existing syntactic Pi-inversion theorem.
It uses the independent strict lower admissible fixed point, the individual
typed rule proofs, a displayed section constructed by derivation induction,
and the compact observation pi(om,om). DerivationRecovery converts the
resulting mere component equalities into actual derivations.

The comparison with CANONICITY/cocan.pdf is a proposed algebraic
reformulation. We have not constructed its full dependent glued model,
lifted Pi and U:U to that model, or obtained its section by CwF initiality.
The generic initiality result below concerns the free derivation algebra.

Checked inversion architecture
------------------------------

DisplayedSyntax.seg defines a displayed algebra over the 17 constructors
of Der, independently of any domain or logical relation. The total algebra
pairs a derivation with its decoration. Section.lift supplies a section of
erasure, and the preserves-* declarations prove constructor preservation.
DisplayedSyntaxInitiality.seg proves pointwise uniqueness among such
constructor-preserving sections. It does not identify distinct derivations
of a judgement or prove initiality of a quotient syntactic CwF.

ScottGlueing.seg instantiates that displayed algebra using the independent
admissible relation. The individual IndependentFundamental* rule modules
supply its operations; IndependentFundamental itself is absent from the
standalone check. ScottGlueing.typing, equality and at-point expose the
resulting section. The legacy filename does not mean that a type-theoretic
Artin glueing construction has been completed.

ScottGlueingSubstitution.seg proves the action on fitting related
environments, identity/composition on their projections, and binder
extension. SyntacticSubstitutions.seg supplies the required purely
syntactic substitution laws.

ScottGlueingInversion.seg constructs a fitting bottom environment with
related identity substitutions. Applying the section to a product equality
and observing pi(om,om) gives the component equalities. Its pi-injectivity
accepts any classifier T and returns actual derivations of

    G |- A = A' : U
    G,A |- B = B' : U.

ScottGlueingKripke.seg defines hereditary points, requiring relatedness
after every typed change of test context. It constructs restriction,
source reindexing, their identity/composition laws, and interchange.
ScottGlueingKripkeInversion.seg derives the same actual inversion through
an explicitly constructed hereditary bottom point.

This hereditary interface is useful, but it does not prove the missing
naturality theorem for an independently built glued model. In particular,
its source reindexing currently uses the existing displayed section.
There is no claim that every fixed-context related point extends to a
hereditary point.

Checks and independence
-----------------------

Run the completed development from DOMAIN:

    python3 UNIT/check.py

The default checks include DisplayedSyntaxCheck.seg, ScottGlueingCheck.seg
and ScottGlueingKripkeCheck.seg. The last excludes ScottGlueingInversion
as well as all earlier syntactic inversion modules. check.py audits the
transitive import closures for these boundaries. The old relation RelC,
its laws and adequacy, and the assembled IndependentFundamental,
IndependentAdequacy, IndependentInversion, IndependentProofRelevant and
IndependentSubjectReduction modules are absent from the new proof drivers.
The existing domain/projector results and raw semantic soundness remain
part of the foundations.

All.seg and the inversion drivers exclude the experimental categorical,
quotient-family and refinement constructions. check.py enforces this
separation. Vendor files and their 40 frozen hashes are unchanged.

Verdict on the glued-model route (20 November 2026)
--------------------------------------------------

1. No Kripke structure is needed for Pi-injectivity. The proof fixes the
   test context Delta; the lambda case only tests arguments in Delta.
   Substitution stability of the fixed-context relation is needed only to
   make the decorations a presheaf in a varying test context. It is not
   provable by rank induction: the function clause is contravariant and
   tests arbitrary arguments of the new context, not substitution
   instances. No counterexample is known either. Making the relation
   itself Kripke would only serve the glued presentation.

2. The cocan analogue glues along Hom(Delta,-) instead of Hom((),-):
   points of Gamma are (rho,s,s') with s,s' : Delta -> Gamma; substitutions
   act by precomposition, so Delta never changes and no Kripke structure
   arises. Contexts, reindexing and extension already exist
   (ScottGlueingSubstitution, ScottGlueingCategory).

3. The point of cocan is proof relevance: A' is arbitrary data in
   |A| -> U_n, so no relation on syntax and no invariance under conversion
   is needed, and the initial map handles all equations. This requires a
   predicative universe U_{n+1} to hold U'. Under U:U, U' would have to
   contain every A', including its own (Girard). This is exactly why the
   Scott proof uses a relation fixed by a semantic code in D.

4. With decorations forced to be that relation, the glued model either
   (a) works on representatives and coincides with the displayed algebra
   already formalised, or (b) works on conversion classes and then needs
   invariance of the relation under judgemental equality. The relation
   is defined through head reduction (and the term relation depends on
   the syntactic type through FunctionTest[T]), so (b) needs either
   Pi-injectivity (circular) or the decoration of the equality derivation,
   i.e. the section itself supplied in advance. It would also need
   initiality of a quotient syntactic CwF for Pi + U:U with eta, which is
   not available; the checked initiality concerns the derivation algebra.

Conclusion: for U:U the domain-plus-relation proof is the right
formulation, and a cocan-style glued model would add only vocabulary.
A proof-relevant glueing belongs to a theory with a universe hierarchy,
where cocan already applies. The experimental categorical files are kept
outside the committed proof route and are not developed further.

The U:U obligation: semantic codes (superseded by the verdict above)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The proposed type decoration over a glued context fibre should be data:
a semantic code a(rho) in D, continuous in the fitting semantic environment
rho, together with the relation supplied at that code by the existing
strict admissible fixed point. This does not require a universe of all
arbitrary predicates. Propositional logical relations are not the problem;
assuming a realizer for every code in advance would assume the desired
section rather than construct it by initiality.

D is the ambient code domain. The existing universe-typing/projector
condition must still be respected when selecting universe codes. The Scott
construction already provides the semantic domain and self-typed universe;
the proposed universe decoration uses that domain and its relation. The
new obligation is to make this code-and-relation interpretation into the
universe of the glued model, including the U:U and Pi clauses. It is not
an additional powerset universe or a size-collapse assumption. The existing
semantic U:U result alone does not prove these decorated-model laws.

For Pi, use the existing semantic Pi constructor and relation clause over
hereditary points, retaining argument conversion, all self/cross comparisons,
and the component equalities visible at pi(om,om).

The main mathematical issue is context naturality. A function clause tests
arguments available in its current syntactic context; a substitution or
new test context can change those available arguments. Hereditary points
make the intended stability explicit, but independent closure, continuity
of the semantic code family, compatibility with conversion, and the
universe/Pi operations still require proofs before a CwF section can be
invoked. They must not be supplied by assuming a global TypeRealizer or
by reusing the already assembled displayed section as the existence proof.

Once those operations and CwF laws are established, syntactic initiality
should supply the computability section. The compact Pi observation would
then extract injectivity from that section. This final categorical route
is the open problem, not a checked result of the current files.

Experimental work, outside the proof route
-----------------------------------------

ArtinGlueing.seg correctly constructs the comma category Set/F on contexts.
ScottGlueingCategory.seg gives a fixed-test Scott instance. The graph
section is a categorical construction on contexts; it is not the missing
computability section of a model of Pi and U:U.

SyntacticFamilies, SyntacticCategory and SyntacticComprehension provide
representative CwF operations, quotient substitution hom-sets and actual
comprehension pullbacks. The presheaf/quotient-family files explore further
basic CwF infrastructure. These have optional drivers and are not imported
by All.seg or needed for the independent inversion result.

PresheafRefinement.seg and RefinedNaturalModel.seg are explicitly labelled
EXPERIMENTAL REPACKAGING. Their global realizers are parameters, making
erasure an isomorphism. ScottQuotientCwF.seg supplies its realizers from
the existing displayed section. These files reorganise an existing result;
they do not establish an Artin-glued model or a new proof of Pi injectivity.
Their isomorphism proofs are not evidence that arbitrary propositional
logical-predicate glueing is uninformative.
