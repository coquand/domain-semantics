dI-domain development, 11 November 2026
======================================

Mathematical account: di-domains.tex / di-domains.pdf.

Completed and included in All.seg:
  ScottMeet / RigidProjector / RigidImage / RigidSubdomain:
    Rigid-projector algebra, Scott images, compactness characterization,
    construction from downward-closed bounded-join-closed predicates.
  DIDomain / DIImage:
    Explicit abstract dI interface; rigid images inherit distributivity
    and the finite-discrete I-axiom, with mere finite presentations.
  RigidSections:
    Section construction over an explicit function-domain interface;
    greatest-section property, monotonicity, intersection, joint meet law.
  StableMaps:
    Small compact-basis tests equivalent to full stability and stable
    comparison. The genuine stable order, not the pointwise order.
    Pointwise comparison suffices below a common stable upper bound.
  StableDirected / DistributiveJoins / StableComplete:
    Directed and bounded joins in stable order are pointwise. Bounded
    completeness needs distributivity in the codomain, not the I-axiom.
  FiniteDiscrete / CompactIdeals:
    Unique finite positions. A nonbottom compact with finite discrete
    lower ideal implies small propositional excluded middle locally.
    Every element below a compact is compact under the literal I-axiom.
    These are derived theorems; no classical global parameter was added.
  StableComposition / PrincipalProjection / StablePatches:
    Stable composition, monotonicity in stable order, rigid restriction
    to principal ideals. The maps x |-> f(x meet a) meet b for compact
    a,b are stable, below f in stable order, and ordinarily compact.
  StableExponential:
    Stable maps between dI-domains form a Scott domain with small compact
    basis. Stable compactness iff ordinary continuous-map compactness,
    for a stable map. The global basis uses stable finite graph
    presentations; density is proved using the direct compact restrictions.
  StableMeet / StableDistributive / StableFiniteIdeals:
    Compatible stable meets are pointwise. Both extra dI axioms hold;
    StableFiniteIdeals.Exponential.domain packages the exponential as DI.
    A subfunction of a finite graph is reconstructed from its finitely
    many clipped outputs. Finite output ideals give a finite enumeration;
    finite tests decide equality and permit duplicate removal.
  FiniteEnumeration / FiniteRange:
    Small finite indices for large types, finite products and mere finite
    choice, decidable finite tests, and duplicate-free finite ranges.
  ScottProduct / DIProduct / StableProduct:
    Products of Scott and dI-domains; stable projections, pairing and
    constants. Product joins and meets are componentwise.
  StableEvaluation / StableCurrying:
    Joint evaluation is stable and continuous. Currying and uncurrying
    are defined for any Scott parameter P and dI-domains D,E, with beta
    and eta identities of stable maps.
  StableSections:
    Actual stable function-domain instance of RigidSections; finite tests
    of section membership for compact basis functions. These are finite
    presentations, not a formalisation of canonical minimal traces.
  SectionContinuity:
    For any Scott parameter P and a monotone family p |-> B^p with each
    fibre evaluation p |-> B^p_x(z) continuous, p |-> R_(B^p)(f) is
    continuous. A continuously varying argument f_p is also allowed.
    No finite-test hypothesis remains: it is proved in StableSections.

  RigidSpace / StableFamilies:
    Rigid projections form the principal dI ideal below identity in the
    stable endomorphism space. Their meets are pointwise. Stable families
    form [D => R(E)], with compatible meets given by fibre intersection.
    StableFamilies.Between.joint proves (B,f) |-> R_B(f) stable.
    StableFamilies.Between.operation proves B |-> R_B stable as a map
    to R([D => E]); computes identifies it with the greatest-section
    construction. No additional finite-test or continuity hypotheses.

  StableInternalComposition / RigidOperations:
    Stable internal composition, and stable composition/evaluation of
    rigid projections. Rigid composition is the meet in R(D).
  StableInputRestriction / StableImageMaps / StableVaryingFamilies:
    Input restriction I_p(f)=f o p is rigid and varies stably with p.
    Stable maps on Fix(p) extend/restrict with beta/eta; stable families
    on that image extend to the ambient family space.
    Q_(p,B)=R_B I_p=I_p R_B is the greatest supported section below f.
    It is stable in (p,B), also jointly with f, and depends only on B's
    values at fixed inputs. This completes varying rigid input domains.
  StableFixpoint / StableProjectorFixpoint:
    The least-fixed-point operator [D=>D] -> D itself is stable.
    Its stages are a directed chain in stable function order.
    Recursive operators on R(D) have rigid stages and a rigid least
    solution, with pointwise limits and earlier-stage absorption.
    Stable feedback through the varying-family operation is supported.
  RigidEmbedding / RigidUniverse:
    Explicit rigid embedding/retraction interface, with stable transport
    of rigid projections. CONDITIONAL universe theorem: given rigid
    embeddings R(D) -> D and [D=>D] -> D, derive stable typing, U:U,
    stable term application with beta, and a stable Pi-code constructor
    with computation, introduction/abstraction and elimination laws.
    This interface adds no global axiom; the construction below now
    supplies both embeddings from an arbitrary seed dI-domain.
  RigidUniverseRecursion:
    Given the code embedding R(D) -> D, a stable code-valued functional
    F:D->R(D) has a stable recursive-type operator mu(F), lying in U.
    mu(F)=encode(F(mu(F))) and it is least among all ambient prefixed
    points. Stages start at bottom, unfold, lie in U, increase, and have
    supremum mu(F). The function embedding is not needed for this theorem.

  RigidEmbeddingComposition / StableConjugation / RigidEmbeddingExponential:
    Identity, composition and image embeddings; stable conjugation.
    Rigid embeddings lift to stable endomorphism spaces by e f r, with
    retraction r g e. Constants and evaluation at bottom give a rigid
    embedding D -> [D=>D]. The code embedding R(D) -> D follows from
    a function-space embedding, since R(D) is a rigid image in [D=>D].
  RigidEmbeddingLaws / RigidLimitOrder / RigidLimitEmbedding:
    Rigid embeddings preserve compactness; their retractions preserve
    bounded joins. Coherent-sequence limits have coordinatewise directed
    and bounded joins, canonical embeddings and their order adjunction.
  RigidLimitDomain / RigidLimitRigid / RigidLimitDI:
    Stage compact elements form a small compact basis of the limit.
    Stage images are downward closed. Finite discrete compact ideals
    transfer from a stage; distributivity holds coordinatewise. The
    limit is an actual dI-domain with canonical rigid stage embeddings.
  OrderedChains / StableExtensional / RigidLimitLaws / RigidLimitMaps:
    Stage projections increase to identity in stable order. Adjacent
    embeddings commute, nested projections absorb, and stable cones
    assemble into stable maps to the limit.
  RigidLimitExponential / RigidLimitExponentialLaws / RigidLimitTail:
    The stable endomorphism space of a rigid limit is stably isomorphic
    to the limit of the endomorphism spaces. Restriction f |-> (r_n f e_n)
    and assembly (f_n) |-> sup_n e_n f_n r_n are inverse stable maps.
    Deleting the first coordinate is also a stable limit isomorphism.
  RigidExponentialTower / RigidReflexiveDomain:
    For EVERY seed dI-domain D, construct D_infinity with a rigid seed
    embedding and D_infinity isomorphic to [D_infinity=>D_infinity].
    Both inverse equations check. This supplies the universe
    representation, U:U, dependent function codes and recursive types
    from the previous theorems. Faithfulness of the seed embedding
    preserves nontriviality. No function/code representation is assumed
    of the seed, and no new global axiom is introduced.

  BooleanDI / BooleanReflexive:
    The two-element chain is a dI-domain under an EXPLICIT LOCAL
    parameter for excluded middle of small propositions. All joins,
    compactness, distributivity and finite discrete ideals are proved.
    Conversely, a Scott structure on this order with bottom false and
    compact top implies that same excluded-middle principle.
    Applying RigidReflexiveDomain gives two distinct represented points;
    the upper seed point is nonbottom and already has tower rank zero.
    This is a conditional concrete seed, not a new global axiom.
  ScottTerminal / StableSyntax / StableSubstitution / StableSyntaxTyping:
    A total stable interpretation of the existing Syntax.Tm, using finite
    product environments with the terminal Scott domain at length zero.
    U and Pi use the represented universe operations; annotated lambda
    denotes encode(f o decode(A)). Renaming and simultaneous substitution
    are proved, including weakening and single substitution. Beta holds
    at arguments fixed by their interpreted type. The dependent-function
    introduction and elimination rules are proved for these denotations.
    This does not yet prove soundness by induction over all Syntax.Der.
  RigidRankStages / ReflexiveSyntax:
    Tower rank means fixedness by the canonical stage projection. Rank
    is downward closed and persists at later stages. Every compact
    observation of an element is captured at some finite tower stage;
    every compact element has a mere finite-rank witness. Postcomposing
    any stable map with the stage projections gives stable approximations
    increasing in stable order to that map. This applies to every term.
    Coordinate n+1 of a represented function is its restriction to stage
    n. In particular, writing p_n=i_n r_n and app(v,x)=decode(v)(x),
      p_n(app(v,p_n(x))) = i_n(v_(n+1)(x_n)).
    These are tower-stage results, not Scott-filter rank identifications.

Remaining obligations:
  1. A nontrivial seed without a classical parameter is NOT supplied.
     BooleanDI makes the necessary classical input explicit for its
     compact nonbottom top. The old Scott filter model has not been
     shown to satisfy the literal finite-discrete dI axioms.
  2. Complete soundness for all Syntax.Der judgements, especially Pi
     congruence and typed function extensionality, remains to be proved
     for the new stable interpretation. The existing Scott-model
     soundness and adequacy do not automatically transfer.
  3. Relate tower ranks to any intended syntactic rank discipline or to
     the earlier Scott-model stages/Proposition 2.27. Stable term
     approximation and the one-step application equation are now proved,
     but do not identify these different constructions. Finiteness of
     the Boolean tower's whole stage carriers has not been formalised.
  4. A constructive event presentation and its relationship to literal
     classical dI-domains remain open. Finite discrete compact lower
     ideals with a nonbottom compact force small excluded middle.

The pointwise formula B_x(f(x)) is not the rigid section projection in
stable order. The construction takes the greatest stable subfunction
that is a section. See the two-element counterexample in the LaTeX.
